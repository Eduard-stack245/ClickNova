ClickNova Tabs Bridge

Only sends names, URLs and favicon addresses of ordinary, non-private website tabs to ClickNova on 127.0.0.1. No passwords, cookies or page contents. Nothing is sent until you enter the pairing key.

The key stays in extension-local storage. ClickNova listens only while the search dialog is open. Closing ClickNova stops the connection. To stop sharing, press Disconnect.
